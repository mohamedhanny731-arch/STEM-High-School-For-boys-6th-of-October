The Beginning

It all began when my two parents admitted for me at this school (Sadat Sity language School "Elezz"), at that moment I was told that I couldn't talk. They were just persuading me to talk till they called my grandma which I spent most of my time with her when I was young and she was able to let me talk. And at that moment started my life out there I spent 10 years in that school full of memories, childish reactions, old friends. I remeber every moment Beginning from arguing with our teachers to let us eat during the session till the happy breaks we spent running and eating and just having fun. I remember my curiuosity waking up every day at 7 o'clock to attend my classes. I have also loved my teachers whom I truely respected.

I found love!

This story I love to keep personal, but I think it is okay since this is a personal website. It all started in preparatory one. I had no idea what love is and has never thought that someone would love that nerdy guy , but it happened and I fell in love. It began when an new girl came up to our class and actually she became friendly and had many friends in a short time. I started feel that feeling called love towards her and I never thought that she would share that feeling and one day i recieved a notification from her congratulating me on my success and scoring the first on my grade. I thought it was just friendly conversation, but going on it seemed like everyone is going to convince it (no one convinced his love at the end btw) and by time we started asking for each other like "Are you going to school today", "How did you do in the exam" and so on. Our chat continued like this for almost a year till the end of 2nd prep and it all changed. I had a plan of admiting to a stem school cuz it was know among my community for being the best btw and i actually left school and moved to another lower level school to have more free time to study for entrance exam of that stem school. And suddenly my chats to her started not being sent, her profile photo disappeared. i couldn't figure out at that moment that she blocked me. I texted her on telegram and she answered and she told me that she will not be ablt to text me back again. I actually respected her decision especially when I grew up cuz such relationship ends up with breakups so the best plan was to keep love till the day I could admit my love in a halal way. Finally, what I am certain about is that neither my love to her nor her love to me has changed and I hope it doesn't change till the day I let her be my wife and I hope Allah make her my wife one day and we came to the end of my story.
